Om sesjonen

Would you like to move your data and workloads to a European public cloud provider, but don't know where to start? Or did you try to find one, and couldn't decide between too many choices? Join this session and get some practical advice on how to get started, things you should consider before making a decision, and what to expect. And maybe just as important: valid reasons for when European public cloud providers aren't the right choice for you, because like always in our business, “it depends” is the right answer to the question whether European public cloud providers are a fit for your needs.
