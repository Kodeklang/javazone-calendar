Om sesjonen

What happens when a weekend experiment accidentally finds a global audience? In this session, I share the raw, unpolished story of transforming a side project into a venture-backed company spanning three continents.

Instead of a "grand master plan," this journey was fueled by the same principles that define great product development: fast iterations, doing things that don't scale, and early feedback loops.

We’ll discuss why the "unscalable" early days are your greatest competitive advantage and how the feedback from your first ten users is more valuable than any market research report. If you’ve ever sat on an idea because it felt "too small" or "too risky," this talk is your invitation to launch. Learn how to iterate your way through fear and build a product that resonates from Oslo to San Francisco.

Passer for

Developers, Product managers, architects, leaders.
