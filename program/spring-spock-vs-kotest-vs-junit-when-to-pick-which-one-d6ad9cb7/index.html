Om sesjonen

Choice of a testing framework to be used in a team is usually overlooked (let’s just take what we have used before, i.e., JUnit). However, nowadays the choice is not that obvious anymore, and the support for testing libraries like Spock and Kotest is much better than many years ago, especially in cooperation with Spring Boot. There are many gotchas you should be aware of.

Spock, Kotest, and JUnit seem to be similar, however, the devil is in the details, and those can sting hard! They differ in terms of features, complexity, flexibility, performance, integration with Spring Boot and Testcontainers, developer friendliness, and maintenance. You could be surprised that switching to another framework can slow down your tests by 30-50%. The choice is tough, and we aren’t limited to only one tool!

Come to my talk to learn key differences, gotchas that can help you to make up your mind which of the tools the best in your context.
